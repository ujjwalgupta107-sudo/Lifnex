System Workflow & Lifecycle

How Lifnex Coordinates Life-Saving Blood In Minutes

From the initial medical requisition to the verified clinical donation, Lifnex synchronizes three critical stakeholders: the Patient/Attending Family, the Voluntary Donor, and the Hospital Transfusion Center.

1

Patient & Attending Family Workflow

Rapid requisition submission with zero bureaucratic friction
Step A: Initiate Emergency Broadcast

Submit blood type, units required, urgency (Normal, Urgent, Critical), and target hospital. Alternatively, use Gemini AI triage to extract parameters from clinical notes.

Step B: Hospital Verification

The target hospital or blood bank verifies the patient case in their coordination portal, upgrading the request to verified status and preventing duplicate or spam requests.

Step C: Live Donor Tracking

Monitor incoming responses in real-time. View accepted donors with estimated times of arrival (ETA) at the hospital without exposing private donor telephone numbers prematurely.

2

Voluntary Donor Workflow

Location-aware matching with complete schedule autonomy
Step A: Registration & Availability

Set verified blood group, preferred travel radius (e.g. 15 km), and toggle the availability switch. When busy or traveling, flip the toggle to pause all emergency pings.

Step B: Compatible Emergency Alert

Receive instant alerts when a compatible patient at a nearby hospital requires blood. Review distance, urgency level, and clinical notes before deciding.

Step C: One-Click Response & Navigation

Accept the request, declare estimated arrival time, and obtain direct GPS hospital navigation. After donating, the hospital certifies the donation, starting a 90-day cooldown period.

3

Hospital & Blood Bank Workflow

Accredited clinical supervision and donation certification
Step A: Verification & Triage

Hospital coordinators review incoming patient requests, validating clinical doctor prescriptions and cross-checking internal blood bank inventory before broadcasting.

Step B: Donor Arrival & Cross-Matching

When responding donors arrive, laboratory staff perform standard pre-donation screening (hemoglobin test, vitals, ABO/Rh cross-matching) under official medical standards.

Step C: Certification & Cooldown

Upon successful collection, the hospital issues a verified digital certificate, increments the donor’s contribution ledger, and automatically switches the donor to a 90-day recovery cooldown.

Clinical Safety First

Strict Healthcare Screening Protocol

Voluntary blood donation is governed by stringent physiological and biochemical safety standards. Lifnex integrates verification touchpoints to ensure donors are never pressured to donate outside clinical deferral windows.

  • ✓ Minimum 90-day cooldown between red blood cell donations.
  • ✓ Mandatory on-site hemoglobin and blood pressure screening.
  • ✓ Full biological cross-matching by certified blood bank pathologists.
